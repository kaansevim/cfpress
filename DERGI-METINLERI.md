# Dergi Metinleri — OJS'e Yapıştırılacak

_Hazırlandı: 19 Ağustos 2026_

Her dergi için iki metin var. OJS'te yeri:

**Settings → Journal → Masthead**

- **Journal Summary** → sitede "Aims and scope" olarak görünür
- **About the Journal** → sitede "About the journal" olarak görünür

Metni olduğu gibi kopyala. Kaydettikten sonra site 10 dakika içinde günceller.

---

## 1. JSS — Journal of Social Solutions

### Journal Summary (→ Aims and scope)

```
The Journal of Social Solutions publishes original research that moves from evidence to action on pressing social problems. The journal is interested in work that does not stop at describing a problem, but tests, evaluates or proposes a response to it — an intervention, a programme, a policy instrument, a service model or a form of community practice.

The journal welcomes work in social work practice and the social work profession; social policy design and evaluation; community health and wellbeing; education and educational equity; the social dimensions of sustainable development and environmental change; poverty, inequality and social protection; migration, displacement and integration; and disability, ageing and care.

Quantitative, qualitative and mixed-methods studies are equally welcome, as are systematic reviews, evaluation studies and carefully documented practice reports. Research that crosses disciplinary boundaries is encouraged, and so is research that reports implementation difficulties and null findings honestly — both are more useful to practitioners than a clean result that cannot be reproduced.

The journal is international in scope. Studies grounded in a single national or local setting are welcome, provided the authors make clear what readers elsewhere can take from the case.

The journal does not publish purely theoretical essays without empirical or applied grounding, advocacy pieces, or descriptive institutional reports without analysis.
```

### About the Journal (→ About the journal)

```
Journal of Social Solutions (JSS) is a peer-reviewed, open access journal published on CF Open, the academic publishing platform of CF Eğitim Danışmanlık ve Organizasyon Limited Şirketi. The language of publication is English.

The journal was founded on a simple observation: a great deal of good social research never reaches the people who could act on it, and a great deal of practical knowledge never reaches the literature. JSS exists to close that gap. It gives equal standing to rigorous evaluation and to well-documented practice, and it asks every author the same question — what should someone do differently after reading this?

Publication frequency: two issues per year, in June and December.
Peer review: double-blind, with at least two independent reviewers.
Access: immediate open access under the CC BY 4.0 licence; authors retain copyright.
Fees: none at any stage — no submission fee, no page charge, no article processing charge.
Screening: all submissions are checked with similarity-detection software before review.
Author identifiers: an ORCID iD is required for every author.
Ethics: the journal follows the guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).
```

## 2. JCF — Journal of Cognitive Formation

### Journal Summary (→ Aims and scope)

```
The Journal of Cognitive Formation studies how knowledge is formed — in minds, in machines, and in the interaction between the two. The journal publishes theoretical and experimental work on cognition, learning, and the mechanisms by which understanding is built, revised and lost.

Topics within scope include learning and memory; concept formation and conceptual change; reasoning and problem solving; language acquisition and language processing; cognitive development across the lifespan; artificial intelligence and machine learning both as models of cognition and as tools for studying it; human–machine interaction and the cognitive effects of intelligent systems; and the cognitive foundations of instruction and educational technology.

The journal publishes experiments, computational models, corpus and behavioural studies, and theoretical papers that make testable claims. Pre-registered studies are encouraged, and replications — including failed ones — are treated as full contributions rather than as secondary material.

Work that connects laboratory findings to learning in real classrooms, workplaces and everyday settings is particularly welcome.

The journal does not publish engineering benchmarks without a cognitive claim, or commentary on artificial intelligence that is not grounded in evidence.
```

### About the Journal (→ About the journal)

```
Journal of Cognitive Formation (JCF) is a peer-reviewed, open access journal published on CF Open, the academic publishing platform of CF Eğitim Danışmanlık ve Organizasyon Limited Şirketi. The language of publication is English.

Research on human cognition and research on machine learning have grown apart, even though both are asking how a system comes to know something. JCF was founded to keep those conversations in the same room. It publishes work from either side, on the condition that it is legible to the other, and it holds both to the same standard of evidence.

Publication frequency: two issues per year, in June and December.

## 3. JECF — Journal of Economic Change and Future

### Journal Summary (→ Aims and scope)

```
The Journal of Economic Change and Future is devoted to structural economic change and its long-run consequences. The journal is concerned with the forces that reshape economies over decades — technology, demography, climate, institutions and urbanisation — rather than with short-term market movements.

Topics within scope include structural transformation and long-run growth; urbanisation, regional and spatial economics; environmental and resource economics and climate policy; the energy transition and its distributional effects; labour markets and technological change; demographic change and ageing economies; institutions, governance and development; and forward-looking public policy analysis.

The journal publishes empirical work at both micro and macro level, theoretical modelling, scenario and simulation studies, policy evaluations, and economic history where it is used to inform the future. Transparent data and reproducible code are expected wherever the underlying material can be shared.

Research on emerging economies is particularly welcome, as is comparative work that sets a single country's experience against a wider pattern.

The journal does not publish market commentary, investment advice or short-term forecasting.
```

### About the Journal (→ About the journal)

```
Journal of Economic Change and Future (JECF) is a peer-reviewed, open access journal published on CF Open, the academic publishing platform of CF Eğitim Danışmanlık ve Organizasyon Limited Şirketi. The language of publication is English.

Economic policy is often made on a horizon of months, while the changes that matter most unfold over decades. JECF was founded to give the longer horizon a dedicated venue — one where the question is not what happens next quarter, but what a structural shift will have meant in twenty years, and what can be decided about it now.

Publication frequency: two issues per year, in June and December.

## 4. JCFo — Journal of Community & Foundations

### Journal Summary (→ Aims and scope)

```
The Journal of Community & Foundations publishes research on civil society, philanthropy and local governance, with particular attention to the part that foundations and voluntary organisations play in social life.

Topics within scope include foundations, endowments and grantmaking practice; nonprofit management, governance and accountability; volunteering and civic participation; local and municipal governance; community development and community organising; social capital and trust; faith-based, diaspora and informal giving; social enterprise and hybrid organisational forms; and the regulation of the third sector.

The journal publishes case studies, organisational ethnography, surveys, comparative and historical research, and evaluations of grantmaking practice. Community-based and participatory research is welcome, including work co-authored with practitioners, provided the methods and the authors' position are described openly.

The journal has a particular interest in philanthropic traditions that are under-represented in the English-language literature, including waqf and other endowment forms, and in the everyday organisational work that keeps small associations running.

The journal does not publish promotional accounts of a single organisation's achievements.
```

### About the Journal (→ About the journal)

```
Journal of Community & Foundations (JCFo) is a peer-reviewed, open access journal published on CF Open, the academic publishing platform of CF Eğitim Danışmanlık ve Organizasyon Limited Şirketi. The language of publication is English.

JCFo was founded to widen that record — to take small associations, local government and unfamiliar giving traditions as seriously as the endowments that already have a literature.

## Not

Metinlerde ISSN geçmiyor — henüz numara alınmadı. Numaralar geldiğinde OJS'te
**Distribution → ISSN** alanına yazman yeterli; sitedeki "Publishing credentials"
sayfası oradan okuyor, metinleri değiştirmene gerek yok.
