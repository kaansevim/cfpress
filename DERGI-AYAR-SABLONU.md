### Settings → Workflow → Submission

**Author Guidelines** (ilk cümle A bölümünden, gerisi aynı)
```
General requirements
- Manuscripts must be original, must not have been published elsewhere, and must not be under consideration by another journal.
- Manuscripts must be written in English; submissions in other languages are not accepted.
- An ORCID iD is required for all authors at submission.

Article types (word limits exclude references, tables and figures)
- Original research article: up to 8,000 words
- Review article: up to 10,000 words
- Letter to the Editor: up to 1,500 words

Manuscript preparation
- The initial submission may be made in your own format, provided the main file is editable (DOCX or LaTeX). PDF-only submissions cannot be processed.
- Once the manuscript is returned for revision or accepted, the revised version must be prepared using the journal's article template: https://cf.org.tr/templates/article-template.docx
- Abstract of 130–150 words, followed by 3–5 keywords.
- Number all headings; cite every table and figure in the text.
- Provide a separate anonymized version for double-blind review.

References
References must follow the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association, 7th edition. In-text citations use the author–date format. Include a DOI for every source that has one.

Ethics
All submissions are screened with similarity-detection software before review. Research involving human participants requires approval from an institutional ethics committee; the approval number and date must be stated in the manuscript. Generative AI tools may not be listed as authors, and any use of such tools must be transparently described in the methods or acknowledgements section.

Charges
The journal does not charge any fee at any stage of the publication process.

Questions may be sent to dergi@cfdanismanlik.com.tr
```

**Submission Checklist**
```
All submissions must meet the following requirements.

- The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration by another journal.
- The manuscript is written in English and the main file is in an editable format (DOCX or LaTeX), not PDF only.
- A title page, an abstract of 130–150 words, and 3–5 keywords are included.
- An anonymized version is provided for double-blind review.
- References follow APA 7th edition, have been checked for accuracy and completeness, and include DOIs where available.
- All tables and figures are numbered, labeled, and cited in the text.
- Permission has been obtained to publish all photographs, datasets and other material provided with the submission.
- Ethics approval information, including the approval number and date, is stated where applicable.
- An ORCID iD is provided for every author.
- A conflict of interest statement is included in the manuscript, covering all authors.
- The corresponding author accepts the CC BY 4.0 licensing terms on behalf of all authors when completing the submission.
```

**Upload Files**
```
Please upload the following files.

1. Main manuscript file (required). An editable file (DOCX or LaTeX) containing the full text, references, tables and figure captions. This file must be anonymized for double-blind review: remove author names, affiliations, acknowledgements and any self-identifying references. Select the file type "Article Text".

2. Title page (required). A separate file listing the article title, all authors with their affiliations and ORCID iDs, the corresponding author's contact details, the conflict of interest statement, funding information, and ethics approval details where applicable. This file is not sent to reviewers.

3. Figures (if applicable). Supply each figure as a separate high-resolution file, named to match its number in the text.

4. Supplementary material (optional). Datasets, appendices, questionnaires or other material that helps the editors and reviewers evaluate your work.

A PDF cannot be accepted as the only manuscript file.
```

**Contributors**
```
Add all authors in the order they should appear in the published article, with affiliation, email and ORCID iD. An ORCID iD is required for every author.

Mark one author as corresponding. Everyone listed here receives the submission confirmation and all editorial decisions.

The author list cannot be changed after submission without the editor's approval.
```

**Details**
```
Enter the title and abstract exactly as they appear in your manuscript.

The abstract must be between 130 and 150 words and should state the purpose, method, main findings and conclusion of the study.

Add 3–5 keywords, entering each one separately.
```

**For the Editors**
```
Add the funding source and grant number if your research was funded, and the ethics committee name, approval number and date if approval was required.

Use the comments box for anything the editor should know, including reviewers you would prefer to be excluded. Only the editorial team sees this.
```

**Review and Submit**
```
Check your details before submitting. You can still edit any section.

Please confirm that the manuscript file is anonymized and that the author list and ORCID iDs are complete and in the right order.

An editor will screen your submission within about a week. You will be notified by email at each stage.
```

**Copyright Notice**
```
Authors retain copyright and grant the journal the right of first publication.

Articles are published open access under the CC BY 4.0 licence: others may share and adapt the work, including commercially, provided the authors and source are credited.

Authors may deposit the published version in repositories or on personal websites, acknowledging first publication in this journal.
```

OJS 3.5 yayin kurulu sayfasini rollerden uretir, cf.org.tr de onu okur.
Her rolu duzenleyip **"Consider role in masthead list"** kutusunu ayarla:

| Rol | Kutu |
|---|---|
| Journal editor, Section editor, Editorial Board Member | **isaretli** |
| Layout Editor, Copyeditor, Proofreader | **isaretli** |
| **Reviewer** | **bos** — hakem adlari sitede gorunmemeli |
| Author, Reader | bos |

  doldur. Site bunlari otomatik gosterir, kodda liste tutulmuyor.
